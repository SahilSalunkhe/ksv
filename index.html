Welcome To KSV Malavali

KSV Malavali is a premium 3BHK luxury villa near Lonavala offering a private pool, premium interiors, spacious bedrooms, indoor and outdoor games and unforgettable experiences for family vacations, friends trips, birthdays and corporate outings.

Nestled in the serene hills of Maval, our villa blends cinematic luxury with the calm of nature — perfect for those who want to escape the city and embrace the finer things in life.
